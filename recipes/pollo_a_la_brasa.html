Pollo a la Brasa

Description: Pollo a la Brasa is a famous Peruvian rotisserie chicken dish featuring

Ingredients:

  • Whole chicken: About 3 to 4 pounds.
  • Aji amarillo paste: A bright yellow Peruvian chili paste adding mild heat and fruity flavor.
  • Aji panca paste: A deep red, mild Peruvian chili paste adding a smoky, sweet depth.
  • Dark soy sauce: Provides umami richness and dark color to the skin.
  • Garlic: Freshly crushed or pureed garlic (several cloves).
  • Vinegar: Red wine vinegar or apple cider vinegar.
  • Oil: Vegetable oil or olive oil.
  • Spices and herbs: Ground cumin, paprika, dried oregano, rosemary, and black pepper.
  • Optional additions:

Steps:

  1. Prepare the marinade: Whisk together the soy sauce, garlic, aji amarillo paste, aji panca paste, vinegar, oil, cumin, paprika, oregano, rosemary, salt, and pepper in a bowl until smooth.
  2. Prep the chicken: Pat the chicken completely dry with paper towels. Gently loosen the skin over the breast and thighs with your fingers so you can rub the marinade directly onto the meat.
  3. Marinate: Coat the entire chicken with the marinade, ensuring you get plenty under the skin and inside the cavity. Cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours, or ideally overnight for the best flavor.
  4. Bring to room temperature: Take the chicken out of the refrigerator about 30 minutes before cooking to take the chill off. This ensures it cooks evenly.
  5. Roast or Grill:
    1. In the oven: Preheat to 400°F (200°C). Place the chicken on a roasting rack in a pan. Roast for about 60 to 75 minutes, basting occasionally with the pan juices, until the internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part of the thigh.
    2. On a rotisserie/grill: Cook over medium-indirect heat for about 1 to 1.5 hours until the internal temperature hits 165°F (74°C).
  6. Rest and serve: Let the chicken rest for 10 to 15 minutes before carving to keep the juices inside.
